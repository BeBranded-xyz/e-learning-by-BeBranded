Exports read the design file: `elearningExport({ design })` replaces `theme` and `logo`. PDFs take the design's text, muted, border, surface and status colors, body and heading fonts and logo; Word files its fonts, text, muted and link colors. `designToExportTheme` gives the surface and status colors too.
