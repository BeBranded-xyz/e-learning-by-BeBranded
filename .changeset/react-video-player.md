`VideoPlayer`, shown by `LessonPlayer` for video lessons: HLS playback (native, or hls.js loaded on demand) with watch verification: played intervals, heartbeats every 15 s and on pause, end, hide and page exit (`keepalive`), resume at the last position, the lesson's seeking and speed limits, and English and French messages. The API client gains `videoHeartbeat`.
