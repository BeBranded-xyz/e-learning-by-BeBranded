`@bebranded/elearning-core/design`: `defineDesign` validates one design file (colors in light and dark in shadcn/ui's vocabulary, fonts, radius, dark mode, brand), `designToModuleCss` and `designToShadcnCss` turn it into CSS variables, `checkDesign` reports text pairs below WCAG AA, `designToExportTheme` gives the exports `#rrggbb` colors and fonts (spec 11).
